title: Launch and Starship
---
Once the eighth section of the [[werft|orbital shipyard]] is finished, all members of your agency currently in Earth orbit are told. The "Launch" button then appears at the shipyard terminal.

## The launch

Clicking "Launch" launches the starship. All members of your agency in Earth orbit see the journey: one after another the planets you have visited pass by, and at the end the Sun stays behind as a small dot. Then the transmitter on [[pluto|Pluto]] answers, and the credits roll. You can cancel at any time with Esc.

All members of your agency get the advancement "Infinite Horizon". Anyone not in the game right now gets it at their next login.

@tip Visit as many planets as possible with your agency beforehand. Every visited planet appears in the launch sequence.

## The starship

After the launch, the starship floats next to your station. It has {{value:vehicles:infinite_horizon:starship.seats}} seats, {{value:vehicles:infinite_horizon:starship.crew_seats}} of them for the crew. Right-clicking seats you in a free place. Whoever gets in first belongs to the crew.

With the inventory key the crew chooses a jump destination. The starship only jumps between orbits; you continue to the planets with the [[landefaehre|lander]]. It needs no fuel: every jump costs {{value:vehicles:infinite_horizon:starship.jump_cost.count}} filled antimatter cell from your inventory. All passengers jump along.

@warning Empty antimatter cells are not enough. Charge them beforehand in the magnetic trap in Jupiter orbit.
