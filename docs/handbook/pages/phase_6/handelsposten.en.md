title: Trading Post
---
At the <item:infinite_horizon:trading_post> you offer other players trades: "Give me X, you get Y". It has up to {{value:logistics:infinite_horizon:trading_post.offers_max}} offers and its own storage with {{value:logistics:infinite_horizon:trading_post.storage_slots}} slots. **Anyone** may trade, including players of other agencies. Only the members of your agency manage storage and offers.

## Setting up offers

1. Set up the trading post and open it with a right-click. The offers are on the left, the storage on the right.
2. Put the goods you want to give away into the storage.
3. In an offer row, click the **left** pattern slot with an item: that is X, what the trader gives you. A left-click takes the whole stack, a right-click counts up by one.
4. Click the **right** pattern slot the same way: that is Y, what the trader gets for it.
5. A left-click without an item clears a pattern slot, a right-click without an item counts down by one.

The patterns are only templates. Trades always use real goods from the storage: next to every offer it says how many more times it works.

@tip What traders give you lands in the storage. Collect it regularly, otherwise the storage fills up at some point and nobody can trade any more.

## Trading

Put X into your inventory and click "Trade" next to the offer. The trading post takes X from your inventory, puts it into the storage and gives you Y. Players of other agencies do not see the storage, only the offers.

## Supplying

Cargo rockets of your agency and your [[agentur|partners]] can choose the trading post as a destination at the [[logistik_computer|logistics computer]]. They only unload items. Fluids stay in the rocket and fly back.

@warning A trading post is never the start of a route. It has no hangar and no fuel tank: plan the return flight with enough fuel from the start terminal.
