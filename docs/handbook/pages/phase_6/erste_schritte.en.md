---
title: First Steps in the Network
---
In the last phase you connect your worlds. Outposts extract resources on their own, cargo rockets bring them to the shipyard in Earth orbit, and there the starship takes shape section by section.

## The sequence

1. **Build the fission reactor.** Uranium from [[pluto|Pluto]] makes fuel rods. The [[kernreaktor|fission reactor]] supplies more power than any other source.
@if infinite_horizon:drilling_rig
2. **Extract.** [[bohrturm|Drilling rig and pump station]] get ores and fluids without you mining yourself. On the geysers of Enceladus and Triton you gain ammonia and nitrogen.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:cargo_terminal
3. **Found outposts.** A [[aussenposten|cargo terminal]] collects everything the producers near it extract. Even while you are away, extraction keeps running.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:cargo_rocket
4. **Fly cargo.** [[frachtraketen|Cargo rockets]] in a terminal's hangar bring items and fluids to other terminals. Drives shorten the flight time.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:logistics_computer
5. **Set up routes.** At the [[logistik_computer|logistics computer]] you decide when which rocket flies what where.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:shipyard_terminal
6. **Supply the shipyard.** In the [[werft|orbital shipyard]] in Earth orbit your starship takes shape in eight sections.
7. **Launch.** After the eighth section you launch the [[sternenschiff|starship]] at the shipyard terminal.
@endif
