title: Mars

Mars is colder and farther away than the Moon, but it has a thin atmosphere and resources the Moon lacks: magnesium, nickel and gypsum.

## The world

- **Gravity:** {{value:planet:mars.gravity}} times Earth's.
- **Temperature:** {{value:planet:mars.temperature.day}} °C by day, {{value:planet:mars.temperature.night}} °C at night.
- **Air:** thin and not breathable. Without a [[raumanzug|suit]] you will not survive long.

## Terrain and biomes

- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_red_plains>:** wide red plains of <item:infinite_horizon:mars_sand>, with magnesium and iron in the ground.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_canyon>:** deep gorges almost down to the bottom of the world. The ore layers lie exposed on the walls.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_volcanic_highlands>:** high mountains of <item:infinite_horizon:mars_rock>. The giant volcanoes stand here, and lava tubes run deep in the rock.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_dry_lake>:** flat basins of <item:infinite_horizon:gypsum_block>.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_polar_cap>:** rare, made of <item:infinite_horizon:dry_ice_block>.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:mars_meteorite_field>:** small craters with <item:infinite_horizon:iron_nickel_meteorite> in the middle. A whole meteorite field is an exploration target and brings research points.

## Ores

@ores infinite_horizon:mars

From <item:infinite_horizon:iron_nickel_meteorite> you get <item:infinite_horizon:raw_nickel> and sometimes <item:minecraft:raw_iron>. You process magnesium and nickel like iron: into dust in the [[zerkleinerer|crusher]], into ingots in the <item:infinite_horizon:electric_furnace>.

@recipes_for infinite_horizon:magnesium_dust infinite_horizon:magnesium_ingot infinite_horizon:nickel_dust infinite_horizon:nickel_ingot

@warning The nights on Mars are icy. Plan your trips so that you are back in the dome before dark.

@tip A canyon saves you a lot of digging: the ores lie open on the walls.
