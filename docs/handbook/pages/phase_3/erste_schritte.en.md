---
title: First Steps on Alien Worlds
---
In the third phase you learn to survive on alien worlds. First with a suit and an oxygen supply, later under protective domes.

## The sequence

1. **Make oxygen.** The [[sauerstoffgenerator|oxygen generator]] makes oxygen from power, the electrolyzer from water.
2. **Fill canisters.** Put [[sauerstoff|oxygen canisters]] into the fill slot and take them out full.
3. **Put on the suit.** Wear all four pieces of the [[raumanzug|pressure suit]]. The chestplate refills from the canisters in your inventory.
4. **Out into the vacuum.** Watch the HUD: the bars show strain and suit oxygen (see [[umwelt|Environment, Strain and Gravity]]).
5. **Build a dome.** A [[kuppel|dome generator]] with power and oxygen creates breathable air for your base. You may also sleep there.
6. **Light without fire.** Outside nothing burns. Use <item:infinite_horizon:electric_lamp> instead of torches, see [[leben_ohne_luft|Living Without Air]].
@if infinite_horizon:landing_beacon
7. **Mark the landing site.** A [[landefeuer|landing beacon]] at your Moon base is the target for all further flights of your agency.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:hydroponics_basin
8. **Grow food.** Under the dome, food grows on [[hydrokultur|compost soil or in the hydroponics basin]]. For the road, the [[weltraumnahrung|food packer]] packs rations.
@endif

@tip Build an oxygen generator with a pressure bottle on the Moon early. Then you can refill your canisters on site.
