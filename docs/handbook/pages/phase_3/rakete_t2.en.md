title: The T2 Rocket
---
Mars and Venus are too far for the first [[rakete|rocket]]. The T2 rocket runs on **hydrogen and oxygen**, has room for {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.seats}} people and needs a bigger launch pad.

## T2 launch pad

The T2 rocket only launches from a platform of {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.pad_size.0}} × {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.pad_size.1}} pad blocks; everything else is as with the [[raumfahrt|launch pad]]. The tower must be as tall as the rocket. A landed T2 rocket also launches without a pad.

## Structure

1. <item:infinite_horizon:rocket_engine_t2> on the centre, {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.fins}} <item:infinite_horizon:rocket_fin_t2> next to it.
2. {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.tanks.0}} to {{value:rocket_parts:rules.2.tanks.1}} <item:infinite_horizon:rocket_tank_t2>, cargo bays if needed.
3. The <item:infinite_horizon:rocket_capsule_t2> on top.

One tank holds {{value:rocket_parts:parts.rocket_tank_t2.fuel_capacity}} mB each of hydrogen and oxygen. The [[elektrolyseur|electrolyzer]] supplies both, and the pump fills the tanks as with T1.

## New destinations

Research **Planetary probes** and send one [[raumfahrt|probe]] each to Mars and Venus. Then both appear on the [[flug|navigation map]]. See [[mars|Mars]] and [[venus|Venus]].
