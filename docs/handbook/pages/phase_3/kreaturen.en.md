---
title: Creatures of the Inner Solar System
---
More lives on the alien worlds than you would think. Most creatures are harmless, some are useful, one is dangerous. With the <item:infinite_horizon:field_scanner> you analyze them: right-clicking a creature gives your agency {{value:research:creature_analysis.fp}} RP the first time for each species.

## Moon

- **Rubble crawler:** lies camouflaged like a stone until you come closer than {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:rubble_crawler.behavior_params.reveal_distance}} blocks. It eats cables and metal blocks outside domes, one every {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:rubble_crawler.behavior_params.eat_interval_ticks|t}}. If you attack it, it fights back. It drops <item:infinite_horizon:metal_concentrate>, which gives metal in the crusher.
- **Shade strider:** shy, flees from light and from you. Follow it long enough and it leads you to ice or ore nearby.

## Mars

- **Dune burrower:** digs invisibly through the sand and is drawn by vibrations, such as a running crusher or the drill of your [[rover|rover]]. It attacks players and drops <item:infinite_horizon:armor_plate>.
- **Dust wraith:** floats peacefully and is immune to arrows. If you get close, it usually leads you to a lava tube or a meteorite field and then vanishes.

## Venus

- **Sulfur snail:** fireproof, leaves a thin <item:infinite_horizon:sulfur_trail> that disappears after a while and now and then lays <item:infinite_horizon:sulfur>. Sulfur makes it breed.
- **Cloud ray:** flies around peacefully. You can tame it with <item:infinite_horizon:sulfur> (not every attempt succeeds), and with a saddle it becomes a flying mount. It flies where you look.

@warning Dune burrowers only come out in the dark. A running machine on Mars draws them from far away.
