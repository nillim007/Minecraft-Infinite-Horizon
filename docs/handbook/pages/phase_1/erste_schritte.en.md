title: First Steps

In the first phase you build a small industry on Earth. By the end you have power, process ores twice as efficiently as in a furnace, make circuits and refine crude oil into kerosene and plastic. That is the foundation for everything that follows.

@image ../images/production-chain.svg

## The sequence

1. **Gather resources.** Above all you need iron, copper and redstone. Also look out for <item:infinite_horizon:bauxite_ore> in savanna, desert and badlands, and for <item:infinite_horizon:titanium_ore> deep underground. More under [[rohstoffe|Resources and Ores]].
2. **Build machine frames.** Almost every machine needs a <item:infinite_horizon:machine_frame>. Build two or three right away.
3. **Generate power.** A <item:infinite_horizon:combustion_generator> burns coal or wood and supplies power. Connect it to your machines with <item:infinite_horizon:copper_cable>. See [[strom|Power]].
4. **Double your ores.** The <item:infinite_horizon:crusher> turns one raw ore into two dusts. Smelt the dust in a furnace or in the <item:infinite_horizon:electric_furnace>. See [[zerkleinerer|Crusher]].
5. **Press plates, rods and wire.** The <item:infinite_horizon:press> reshapes ingots with a mold. You will constantly need copper wire and iron plates. See [[presse|Press]].
6. **Smelt silicon.** <item:infinite_horizon:quartz_sand> from beaches or river banks becomes <item:infinite_horizon:silicon> in the electric furnace.
7. **Build the assembly table.** From now on all important components come out of the <item:infinite_horizon:assembly_table>. See [[montagetisch|Assembly Table]].
8. **Pump crude oil.** Oil puddles lie on the surface of deserts and badlands. A <item:infinite_horizon:fluid_pump> sucks the crude oil into a <item:infinite_horizon:fluid_tank>. See [[fluide|Fluids and Pipes]].
9. **Build the refinery.** The first multiblock turns crude oil into kerosene and plastic. See [[raffinerie|Refinery]].
10. **Make circuits.** Silicon, copper wire and plastic give the <item:infinite_horizon:circuit> in the assembly table, the most important component of the mod.
11. **Split water.** The <item:infinite_horizon:electrolyzer> turns water into hydrogen and oxygen. See [[elektrolyseur|Electrolyzer]].
12. **Get faster.** With circuits you build [[upgrades|upgrades]] that speed up your machines or make them more efficient.
