The <item:infinite_horizon:press> reshapes ingots. The **mold** in the mold slot decides which shape comes out. The mold is not used up.

- With a <item:infinite_horizon:press_mold_plate>, 1 ingot becomes 1 plate.
- With a <item:infinite_horizon:press_mold_rod>, 1 ingot becomes 2 rods.
- With a <item:infinite_horizon:press_mold_wire>, 1 ingot becomes 3 wire.
