title: Resources and Ores
---
Besides the vanilla ores, Infinite Horizon brings three new resources to Earth. On top of that there is crude oil deep in the ground.

## Where to find them

@ores minecraft:overworld

## Bauxite – Earth's aluminium

<item:infinite_horizon:bauxite> is the raw form of aluminium. You find it high up, between y {{value:planet:overworld.ores.0.y_min}} and {{value:planet:overworld.ores.0.y_max}}, but only in hot, dry areas: savanna, desert and badlands. A stone pickaxe is enough to mine it.

## Titanium – deep and hard

<item:infinite_horizon:titanium_ore> lies deep in the ground, below y 0 mostly as the deepslate variant. You need at least an **iron pickaxe**, otherwise you get nothing. You will need titanium for rockets in large amounts, so remember good spots.

## Quartz sand – for silicon

<item:infinite_horizon:quartz_sand> lies in small disks on beaches and river banks, much like clay. The electric furnace turns it into <item:infinite_horizon:silicon>.

## Crude oil

@if infinite_horizon:crude_oil
Crude oil lies in **oil puddles** on the surface of deserts and badlands: round, shiny black hollows with a radius of {{value:planet:overworld.features.0.params.radius.0}} to {{value:planet:overworld.features.0.params.radius.1}} blocks, up to {{value:planet:overworld.features.0.params.depth.1}} blocks deep in the middle. About one in {{value:planet:overworld.features.0.params.rarity_chunks}} chunks has one. How to pump crude oil is explained under [[fluide|Fluids and Pipes]].
@endif

@tip You can smelt bauxite directly in a furnace. If you crush it first, though, you get twice as much aluminium.
