title: Crusher
---
The <item:infinite_horizon:crusher> is the first machine you should build. It grinds the raw forms of metals into **two** dusts. Smelt the dust and every ore gives you two ingots instead of one.

@machine infinite_horizon:crusher

@image ../images/screenshots/gui-crusher.png | The crusher window: energy on the left, input, progress arrow, four output slots and the upgrade slots bottom right.

## And then?

You smelt dust in a normal furnace or in the [[elektroofen|electric furnace]]:

@recipes_type smelting

@tip Put a hopper on top of the crusher and a second one below it into an electric furnace. Then raw ore turns into ingots all by itself.
