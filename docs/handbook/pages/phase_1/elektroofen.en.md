---
title: Electric Furnace
---
The <item:infinite_horizon:electric_furnace> smelts everything a normal furnace smelts, but uses power instead of fuel. It can also do things no furnace can, for example <item:infinite_horizon:silicon> from quartz sand.

@machine infinite_horizon:electric_furnace

@image ../images/screenshots/gui-electric-furnace.png | The electric furnace window with two input slots.

## Everything from the normal furnace

The electric furnace accepts every vanilla smelting recipe. That includes the ingots from Infinite Horizon:

@recipes_type smelting

@note The electric furnace takes the same time for every recipe and gives no experience. It cannot do blast furnace or smoker recipes.
