title: Advancements

The Infinite Horizon advancements are on their own tab in the advancements menu (key `L`). They trace the journey from the first research terminal to the launch of the starship.

| Advancement | How to get it |
| --- | --- |
| Infinite Horizon | Take the <item:infinite_horizon:engineers_handbook> in hand. |
| Ground Station | Place a <item:infinite_horizon:research_terminal>. |
| First Signal | A probe of your agency reaches the Moon. |
| One Small Step | Set foot on the Moon. |
| Red Earth | Set foot on Mars. |
| Three Minutes of Hell | Descend below the cloud layer on Venus (below y {{value:suits:venus_heat_timer.applies_below_y}}) and make it back up. |
| Air of Your Own | A dome generator of your agency runs for the first time. |
| Home in Orbit | Place a <item:infinite_horizon:station_core>. |
| Sun in a Bottle | A [[fusionsreaktor|fusion reactor]] of your agency generates power. |
| Under the Ice | Dive below the ice shell into the water on Europa. |
| At the Edge | Set foot on Pluto. |
| The Transmitter | Defeat the sentinel at the transmitter. |
| Network | {{value:misc:advancements.by_id.network.active_routes}} routes of your agency are active at the same time: each has at least one rocket, and none of them reports "out of fuel". |
| Infinite Horizon (starship) | Launch the starship at the [[werft|orbital shipyard]]. |

## For the whole agency

Advancements triggered by a machine or an event go to **all members** of your agency: First Signal, Air of Your Own, Sun in a Bottle, Network and the starship. Whoever is not online right now gets them the next time they enter the world.

When the starship launches, all [[agentur|partner]] agencies that delivered something to the shipyard get the advancement too.

@tip The server writes every advancement with time and player name into the file `logs/infinite_horizon-progress.log`. It stays on the server and is not sent anywhere.
