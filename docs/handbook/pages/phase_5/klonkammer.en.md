---
title: The Clone Chamber
---
Far out, the way back from world spawn is very long. The <item:infinite_horizon:clone_chamber> brings you back to a place of your choice after death, even in another dimension.

## How it works

1. Put the clone chamber in a safe place, for example under a dome or in a station.
2. Connect it to power. It needs power all the time, even when nobody is dying.
3. Put <item:infinite_horizon:microbe_culture> into the input slot. Microbial cultures come from the hydrothermal vents of [[europa|Europa]].
4. Right-clicking the chamber binds you to it. Every player has exactly one chamber; a new binding replaces the old one.

If you die, you wake up above the chamber if it has power and contains at least one culture. It uses up one culture doing so. The chamber does not need to be loaded for this.

@tip A stack of cultures lasts for many trips. Top up the chamber before you fly to the outer moons.
