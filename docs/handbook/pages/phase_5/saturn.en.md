title: Saturn Orbit
---
Saturn lies so far out that a T2 rocket only gets there with the [[fernflug|long-range module]]. As with Jupiter you arrive in **Saturn orbit** above the gas giant. From there the [[landefaehre|lander]] takes you to the moons [[titan|Titan]] and [[enceladus|Enceladus]].

## Tier 5

As soon as a member of your agency sets foot in Saturn orbit, research tier 5 opens: the outer system.

## Helium from Saturn

A [[jupiter|gas collector]] in Saturn orbit collects {{value:planet:saturn_orbit.gas_collector_output.amount}} mB of helium every {{value:planet:saturn_orbit.gas_collector_output.ticks}} ticks.

## Ring debris

Saturn's rings scatter debris. In a debris shower, chunks strike within {{value:events:infinite_horizon:ring_debris.effects.impact_radius}} blocks of every player every {{value:events:infinite_horizon:ring_debris.effects.impact_interval_ticks|t}}, like a meteor shower but without ore. Only a dome's <item:infinite_horizon:dome_shield_module> intercepts them.

@warning Without a shield module a debris shower hits your station too.

@tip Do not fall below the station in Saturn orbit: as with Jupiter, the nearest station core brings you back, and that hurts.
