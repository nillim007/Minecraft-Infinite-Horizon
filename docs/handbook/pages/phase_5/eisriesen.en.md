---
title: Uranus and Neptune Orbit
---
Uranus and Neptune are the ice giants at the edge of the planetary system. As with Jupiter and Saturn you only reach their orbits, and only with the [[fernflug|long-range module]]. From Neptune orbit the [[landefaehre|lander]] flies to the moon [[triton|Triton]].

## The gas collector at the ice giants

A [[jupiter|gas collector]] in Uranus or Neptune orbit collects {{value:planet:uranus_orbit.gas_collector_output.amount}} mB of methane every {{value:planet:uranus_orbit.gas_collector_output.ticks}} ticks. With the **Mode** button in the GUI you switch to hydrogen and back.

@tip Empty the tank before you switch. As long as the other gas is still inside, the collector cannot add anything.

@warning Whoever falls below the station does not land on the planet, but on the nearest station core, and takes damage.
