Pluto is the most distant destination in the solar system. The rocket needs the [[fernflug|long-range module]] and lands directly on the surface. It is {{value:planet:pluto.temperature.day}} °C cold, and gravity is {{value:planet:pluto.gravity}} g.

## Equipment

A [[hitzeanzug|tier 2 suit]] with a [[tiefraumanzug|heating module]] is enough; the deep space suit is better.

@warning Without a heating module you freeze to death on Pluto in any suit below tier 3.

## Areas

- **Heart glacier**: a wide plain of <item:infinite_horizon:pluto_nitrogen_ice> within {{value:planet:pluto.generator.biome_source.radius}} blocks of the landing site at X/Z 0/0. The [[sender|transmitter]] lies beneath it.
- **Ice mountains** of <item:infinite_horizon:pluto_water_ice>.
- **Dark highlands**, covered with <item:infinite_horizon:pluto_tholin>.

## Uranium

Only the dark highlands hold <item:infinite_horizon:pluto_uranium_ore>, between y {{value:planet:pluto.ores.0.y_min}} and {{value:planet:pluto.ores.0.y_max}}. You need an iron pickaxe. The crusher turns <item:infinite_horizon:raw_uranium> into <item:infinite_horizon:uranium_dust>, and only the electric furnace smelts that into <item:infinite_horizon:uranium_ingot>.

@recipes_for infinite_horizon:uranium_dust infinite_horizon:uranium_ingot

@tip The first uranium ingot is a step towards the last research tier. Bring enough raw uranium for a whole furnace load.
