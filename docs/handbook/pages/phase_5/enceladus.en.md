Enceladus is a small, icy moon of Saturn without air. At {{value:planet:enceladus.temperature.day}} °C it is so cold that you need a [[hitzeanzug|tier 2 suit]]. At {{value:planet:enceladus.gravity}} g, gravity is almost zero.

## Areas

- **Snow fields** of <item:infinite_horizon:enceladus_snow>.
- **Tiger stripes** of <item:infinite_horizon:enceladus_ice>, with one ammonia geyser per chunk.

## Ammonia geysers

Every {{value:items:infinite_horizon:ammonia_geyser.eruption_interval_ticks|t}} an <item:infinite_horizon:ammonia_geyser> erupts and shoots a fountain {{value:items:infinite_horizon:ammonia_geyser.fountain_height}} blocks high. Whoever stands in the fountain is hurled upwards at {{value:items:infinite_horizon:ammonia_geyser.entity_velocity_up}} blocks per tick. Geysers cannot be mined.

@tip In the low gravity you float for a long time after a geyser jump. That is a quick way onto high ice ridges.

@warning Do not build directly above a geyser. The fountain lifts everything standing above it.

## The geyser moth

The **geyser moth** circles around the geysers. It is harmless; where you see it, a geyser is not far.
