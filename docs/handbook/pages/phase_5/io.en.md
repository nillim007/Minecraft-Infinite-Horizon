Io is Jupiter's innermost large moon and full of volcanoes. The surface of <item:infinite_horizon:io_crust> is cold ({{value:planet:io.temperature.day}} °C), but there is lava everywhere. Gravity is {{value:planet:io.gravity}} g, and Jupiter's radiation is at level {{value:planet:io.radiation}}.

## Equipment

- Everywhere: a space suit with a <item:infinite_horizon:radiation_shield>.
- Near lava the temperature rises to {{value:planet:io.zones.0.temperature}} °C. There you need a [[hitzeanzug|heat suit]] with a radiation shield.

## Areas

- **Sulfur plains**, **lava lakes** and **volcanic fields**. Volcanic vents stand in the volcanic fields; whoever sets foot on one earns their agency research points as an exploration target.
- <item:infinite_horizon:io_sulfur_ore> is everywhere in the crust and gives sulfur.

## The sulfur salamander

The sulfur salamander lives in the lava lakes and at the volcanic vents. It is fireproof and shoots sulfur globs at you from afar ({{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:sulfur_salamander.damage}} damage per hit). When defeated it sometimes drops a <item:infinite_horizon:heat_scale>.

@uses infinite_horizon:heat_scale

## Volcanic eruption

If a volcanic vent is near a player, it can erupt. You are warned in advance. During the eruption, chunks of sulfur ore rain down within {{value:events:infinite_horizon:volcanic_eruption.effects.radius}} blocks of the vent. Distance is the only protection.

@warning The chunks hit hard. Leave the vent's radius as soon as the warning comes.

@tip After the eruption, fresh sulfur ore lies around in the open. Collect it before you move on.
