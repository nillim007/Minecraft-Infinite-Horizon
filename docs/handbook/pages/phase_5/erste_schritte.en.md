---
title: First Steps in the Outer System
---
Research tier 4 opens the solar system beyond the Moon, Mars and Venus. Every destination there demands its own equipment: heat, cold and radiation are harsher than in ring 1.

## The sequence

1. **Build the advanced probe.** The "Advanced probe" node unlocks the <item:infinite_horizon:advanced_probe> and all destinations in ring 2. It launches from the [[raumfahrt|launch pad]] like the simple probe and also reaches the old destinations.
2. **To Mercury.** [[merkur|Mercury]] has lithium. Take a heat suit and a radiation shield.
3. **Process lithium.** Lithium salt becomes ingots, and those become [[lithium|lithium batteries]], the basis of all devices of the fourth phase.
@if infinite_horizon:asteroid_rock
4. **Into the asteroid belt.** The [[asteroiden|asteroids]] hold platinum and iridium, which make iridium chips. On [[ceres|Ceres]] an ice volcano waits as an exploration target.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:gas_collector
5. **To Jupiter.** In [[jupiter|Jupiter orbit]] the gas collector gathers hydrogen. The lander takes you on to the volcanic moon [[io|Io]].
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:europa_ice
6. **Under Europa's ice.** With ice drill and dive module you reach the ocean of [[europa|Europa]]. You need the microbial cultures from the vents for the [[klonkammer|clone chamber]].
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:titan_ice_rock
7. **To Saturn.** With the long-range module you reach [[saturn|Saturn orbit]] and open tier 5. The lander takes you to [[titan|Titan]] and [[enceladus|Enceladus]].
8. **Antimatter.** The [[magnetfalle|magnetic trap]] in Jupiter orbit fills antimatter cells.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:triton_ice
9. **To the ice giants.** In [[eisriesen|Uranus and Neptune orbit]] you collect methane. For [[triton|Triton]] you need the [[tiefraumanzug|deep space suit]] with a heating module.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:transmitter_core
10. **Pluto and the transmitter.** [[pluto|Pluto]] has uranium. Beneath the heart glacier the [[sender|transmitter]] waits with its sentinel, guarding the star chart.
@endif

@recipes_for infinite_horizon:advanced_probe

@tip The advanced probe costs more than the simple one. Send it first to the destination whose resources you need next.
