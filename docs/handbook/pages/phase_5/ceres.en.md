Ceres is the largest body in the asteroid belt: a dwarf planet with {{value:planet:ceres.gravity}} g, cold ({{value:planet:ceres.temperature.night}} to {{value:planet:ceres.temperature.day}} °C) and without air. A pressure suit is enough.

- **Ceres plains** of <item:infinite_horizon:ceres_rock>.
- **Salt flats**, covered with <item:infinite_horizon:salt_crust>.

## The ice volcano

Near the landing site at X/Z 0/0 stands a cone of ice, the only ice volcano on Ceres. Whoever sets foot on it earns their agency research points as an exploration target.

@warning On Ceres nothing grows without a dome, and water freezes at once. Take oxygen for the whole trip.

@tip The volcano's ice is a convenient water supply for a base on Ceres.
