---
title: Magnetic Trap and Antimatter
---
In Jupiter's strong magnetic field, antiparticles can be trapped. That is why the <item:infinite_horizon:magnetic_trap> only works in [[jupiter|Jupiter orbit]].

## How it works

1. Build the magnetic trap in your station in Jupiter orbit and connect it to plenty of power.
2. Put an empty <item:infinite_horizon:antimatter_cell> into the input slot.
3. After a long operation the filled cell lies in the output slot. Filled cells shimmer.

@machine infinite_horizon:magnetic_trap

@recipes_for infinite_horizon:magnetic_trap infinite_horizon:antimatter_cell

@tip The first filled cell is a step towards the last research tier. Start early, the operation takes a long time.

@warning Without power the progress stops. Provide a battery or a fusion reactor in the station.
