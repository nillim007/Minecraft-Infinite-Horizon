title: Titan

Titan is the only moon with a dense atmosphere. You cannot breathe it, but the pressure is normal, and it is {{value:planet:titan.temperature.day}} °C cold. Gravity is {{value:planet:titan.gravity}} g. A pressure suit is enough.

## Areas

- **Methane lakes**: up to y {{value:planet:titan.sea_level}} the basins are filled with liquid <item:infinite_horizon:methane>.
- **Dunes** of <item:infinite_horizon:titan_dune_sand>.
- **Ice mountains** of <item:infinite_horizon:titan_ice_rock>.

Instead of rain, **methane rain** falls on Titan.

## Gliding

In the dense air you sink more slowly with elytra (sink rate ×{{value:planet:titan.special.elytra_sink_rate_multiplier}}). From an ice mountain that gets you far across the lakes.

@tip Jump off a high peak with elytra. On Titan you glide farther than on Earth without fireworks.

## The methane skimmer

The **methane skimmer** runs across the methane lakes. It flees as soon as you come closer than {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:methane_skimmer.behavior_params.flee_player_distance}} blocks. When hunted it gives a <item:infinite_horizon:skimmer_leg>.

@uses infinite_horizon:skimmer_leg

@warning Whoever falls into a methane lake sinks as in water. Stay near the shore and take enough oxygen.
