Triton is the coldest place you can reach: {{value:planet:triton.temperature.day}} °C. You cannot breathe its thin air. Gravity is {{value:planet:triton.gravity}} g.

## Equipment

No suit alone is enough here. You need the [[tiefraumanzug|deep space suit]] with a heating module.

@warning Without a heating module you freeze to death on Triton even in the deep space suit.

## Areas

- **Cantaloupe terrain**: rolling hills of <item:infinite_horizon:triton_ice>.
- **Polar cap** and **geyser fields**, both rare. <item:infinite_horizon:nitrogen_geyser> stand in the geyser fields. They cannot be mined.

## The frost lurker

The **frost lurker** lies in wait in the ice. It is invisible until you come closer than {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:frost_lurker.behavior_params.emerge_distance}} blocks; then it bursts out and attacks ({{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:frost_lurker.damage}} damage). When defeated it sometimes drops <item:infinite_horizon:insulation_fabric>, the material for the deep space suit.

@uses infinite_horizon:insulation_fabric

@tip Walk slowly across the ice in the dark. If a frost lurker bursts out, you have a moment before it reaches you.
