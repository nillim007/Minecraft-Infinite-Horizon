The deep space suit is the tier 3 space suit. Like the [[hitzeanzug|heat suit]] it protects against acid and heat, withstands {{value:suits:deep_space_suit.temp_min}} to {{value:suits:deep_space_suit.temp_max}} °C and shields radiation up to level {{value:suits:deep_space_suit.radiation_max}}. As armor it is as strong as netherite.

## The heating module

The <item:infinite_horizon:heating_module> goes into the chestplate and shifts the lower temperature limit by {{value:suits:infinite_horizon:heating_module.temp_min_bonus}} °C. You need it with the deep space suit on [[triton|Triton]], and with the heat suit on Pluto as well.
