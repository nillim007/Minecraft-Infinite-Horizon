---
title: The Transmitter
---
Beneath the heart glacier on [[pluto|Pluto]] lies a hall of <item:infinite_horizon:ancient_alloy> that nobody you know has built. The alloy cannot be mined. The <item:infinite_horizon:transmitter_core> stands in the middle.

## The way in

Near the landing site a ring of ancient alloy marks the entrance. From there a stair tunnel of ice leads down to an opening in the south wall of the hall. Whoever enters the hall earns their agency research points as an exploration target.

## The signal sentinel

Right-clicking the transmitter core wakes the **signal sentinel**, a boss with {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.hp}} health points:

1. As long as it has more than {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.phases.0.hp_to|%}} of its health, it strikes ({{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.damage}} damage) and every {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.phases.0.attacks.1.interval_ticks|t}} it knocks everything within {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.phases.0.attacks.1.knockback_radius}} blocks away with a shockwave.
2. Below that, it summons {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.phases.1.summon.count}} frost lurkers and hits you with a beam if you stay in its gaze too long.

Every agency has its own sentinel. After a victory, the transmitter stays silent for your agency for {{value:creatures:infinite_horizon:signal_sentinel.spawn.resummon_cooldown_ticks|t}}.

@warning The sentinel is the strongest enemy in the solar system. Come with full armor, healing potions and reinforcements.

## The star chart

When defeated, the sentinel drops your agency's <item:infinite_horizon:star_chart>. Every agency owns at most one. The chart does not burn and does not despawn when it lies around. If it is destroyed anyway, for example in the void, you can win a new one at the next victory.

@tip Keep the star chart in a chest at your base. With it you will later go beyond the solar system.
