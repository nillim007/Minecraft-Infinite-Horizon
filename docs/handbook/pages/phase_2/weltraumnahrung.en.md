title: Space Food

On long journeys inventory space is scarce. The <item:infinite_horizon:food_packer> presses {{value:agriculture:infinite_horizon:space_ration.inputs}} food items of any kind into a <item:infinite_horizon:space_ration>. The ration fills you as much as all ingredients together, at most {{value:agriculture:infinite_horizon:space_ration.nutrition_max}} hunger points and {{value:agriculture:infinite_horizon:space_ration.saturation_max}} saturation. Rations stack up to {{value:agriculture:infinite_horizon:space_ration.max_stack}}.

The research node "Space food" unlocks the food packer, see [[forschung|Research]].

@machine infinite_horizon:food_packer

## How it works

1. Set up the food packer and connect it to power.
2. Put one food item into each input slot. They may be different.
3. After one operation the ration lies in the output slot. The tooltip shows how much it fills.

@recipes_for infinite_horizon:food_packer

@tip Use filling ingredients like bread, baked potatoes or steak. They make a ration that almost completely satisfies your hunger.
