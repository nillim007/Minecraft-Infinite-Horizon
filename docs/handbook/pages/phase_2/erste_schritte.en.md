title: First Steps
---
In the second phase you leave Earth. The goal is the Moon: new resources wait there, but also the first real dangers.

## The sequence

1. **Set up the research terminal.** It opens research tier 1, see [[forschung|Research]].
2. **Collect research points.** Take samples of ores with the <item:infinite_horizon:sample_tool> and evaluate them in the <item:infinite_horizon:analyzer>. Research the pressure suit first.
3. **Send a probe to the Moon.** Build a [[raumfahrt|launch pad]] with fuel pump and control panel and launch a probe. Only once it has arrived is the Moon a rocket destination.
4. **Build and fuel the rocket.** Engine, fins, tanks and capsule on the pad, kerosene and oxygen into the pump, see [[rakete|The Rocket]].
5. **Know the dangers.** Read what awaits you on alien worlds: [[umwelt|Environment, Strain and Gravity]].
6. **Fly to the Moon.** Sit in the capsule wearing your suit, open the rocket computer with the inventory key and press "Launch". On the navigation map you choose the Moon and the landing site, see [[flug|The Flight]].
7. **Explore the Moon.** Highlands, mare and craters have their own ores, and research points wait in the lava tubes of the mare. See [[mond|The Moon]]. [[strom_im_all|Solar panels]] supply power there.
8. **Fill the atlas.** The [[atlas|planetary atlas]] shows which biomes and exploration targets your agency already knows. A helmet lamp in the [[raumanzug|pressure suit]] helps in dark craters.

@tip Set up a [[landefeuer|landing beacon]] at your base before you take off. Then the return flight brings you home instead of to world spawn.
