---
title: The Planetary Atlas
---
You get the <item:infinite_horizon:planetary_atlas> as a gift on your first login. **Right-click** opens it. What it contains belongs to your agency: all members see the same atlas.

## Layout

On the left are all destinations, ordered by their distance from the Sun. On the right you see for the selected destination:

1. **Status:** unknown, probe arrived ([[raumfahrt|probe]]) or visited.
2. **Discoverer:** the first agency whose member set foot on the destination. Whoever arrives first is announced to all players.
3. **Environment values**, as soon as a probe has arrived: gravity, temperature by day and night, pressure and radiation.
4. **Visited biomes:** they count as soon as a member travels there.
5. **Exploration targets** with coordinates, e.g. the lava tubes on the [[mond|Moon]].
6. **Landing beacons** of your agency, see [[landefeuer|The Landing Beacon]].

@tip Lost it? You craft a new atlas at the crafting table. It immediately shows everything your agency knows again.
