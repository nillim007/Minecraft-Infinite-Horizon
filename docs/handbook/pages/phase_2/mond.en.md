title: The Moon

The Moon is the first destination of your space program. It is close but hostile: no air, vacuum and strong temperature swings between day and night. In return, aluminium and titanium lie here in large amounts.

## The world

- **Gravity:** {{value:planet:moon.gravity}} times Earth's. You jump higher and fall more gently (see [[umwelt|Environment, Strain and Gravity]]).
- **Temperature:** {{value:planet:moon.temperature.day}} °C at noon, {{value:planet:moon.temperature.night}} °C at night. Always {{value:planet:moon.biome_temperature_overrides.moon_polar_crater}} °C in the polar crater.
- **Sky:** black, the stars shine even by day, and Earth hangs large in the sky.
- **Day and night** run as on Earth.

## Terrain and biomes

- **<item:infinite_horizon:moon_highlands>:** rugged highlands of <item:infinite_horizon:moon_regolith>.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:moon_mare>:** flat, dark plains of <item:infinite_horizon:moon_basalt>. The lava tubes run here too, long straight tunnels deep in the rock.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:moon_great_crater>:** home of the great craters with a central peak. Inside them you find twice as much ore.
- **<item:infinite_horizon:moon_polar_crater>:** rare and freezing cold, with patches of <item:infinite_horizon:moon_ice>. There is no solar power here.

Small craters are scattered everywhere. Below the surface the Moon consists of <item:infinite_horizon:moon_rock>.

## Ores

@ores infinite_horizon:moon

<item:infinite_horizon:moon_aluminum_ore> needs at least a stone pickaxe, <item:infinite_horizon:moon_titanium_ore> an iron pickaxe, just as on Earth. <item:infinite_horizon:moon_ice> drops ordinary ice when mined.

## Monsters

At night and in the dark, zombies, skeletons, spiders and creepers show up, but less often than on Earth. They wear breathing masks and need no oxygen.

@warning Without protection you survive on the Moon only for seconds: oxygen, pressure and temperature build up strain immediately.

@tip Land at night or at dusk if you can. It is cold then, but you are spared the midday heat of {{value:planet:moon.temperature.day}} °C.
