title: Space Weather Station
---
The <item:infinite_horizon:space_weather_station> watches the Sun and the sky. As long as it has power, it warns all members of your agency in the same world {{value:events:event_planning.warning_ticks_with_station|t}} before an event instead of only {{value:events:event_planning.warning_ticks_without_station|t}} before. It works everywhere except on Earth and needs an owner.
