title: The Lander
---
A rocket is built for long distances. For the short way between your station and the ground below there is the <item:infinite_horizon:lander>. It has {{value:spaceflight:lander.seats}} seats and a tank with {{value:spaceflight:lander.tank_per_component}} mB of hydrogen and as much oxygen.

## Setting down and fuelling

1. Right-clicking a block in orbit or a [[stationsmodule|docking bay]] with the lander sets it down, like a boat. That does not work on a planet.
2. Fuel it like a rocket: through the slot pair in the window, or on a docking bay with tanks next to it.
3. Sit down, open the window with the inventory key and press "Launch".

## Where it flies

The lander only commutes between an orbit and the bodies below: from Earth orbit to Earth and the Moon, from Mars orbit to Mars, from Venus orbit to Venus. The navigation map only shows these destinations. A flight costs the launch and the landing, but no transfer, see [[rakete|Fuel]].

@warning The lander does not launch from Earth's surface. Bring it into orbit with a rocket and leave it there.

@tip A lander on a docking bay with a tank depot next to it is quickly full again after every return.
