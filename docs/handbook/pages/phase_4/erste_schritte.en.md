---
title: First Steps in Orbit
---
In the fourth phase you leave the surfaces behind and build a space station. It is the hub for all further flights: a launch from the station costs no fuel for the ascent.

## The sequence

1. **Open tier 3.** Obtain helium, magnesium and tungsten, see [[forschung|Research]].
2. **Fly into orbit.** A T2 rocket reaches Earth orbit. It is always known on the navigation map, see [[orbit|The Orbit]].
3. **Place the station core.** Take a <item:infinite_horizon:station_core> along. A right-click from the capsule places it right below the rocket, see [[raumstation|The Space Station]].
4. **Get out safely.** The core's built-in dome supplies you with air. With <item:infinite_horizon:magnet_boots> in your boots you walk on the station as on Earth.
@if infinite_horizon:docking_bay
5. **Expand the station.** Docking bay, tank depot, solar wing and habitat module turn the station into a hub, see [[stationsmodule|Station Modules]].
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:lander
6. **Commute.** With the [[landefaehre|lander]] you fly between station and Earth or Moon without a big rocket.
@endif
@if infinite_horizon:fusion_reactor
7. **Power for the station.** A [[fusionsreaktor|fusion reactor]] supplies power for the dome, fabricators and gravity plates.
@endif

@tip Take a filled suit and oxygen canisters along. Until the core's dome is up, you are in a vacuum.
