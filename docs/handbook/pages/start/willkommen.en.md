Welcome, engineer! You stand at the start of a long journey: from a small workshop on Earth to the **orbital shipyard**, where you finally build the starship.

Infinite Horizon takes you through the **real solar system**. Every step builds on the one before: first you need power and machines, then fuel, then a rocket. On the Moon you learn to survive without air, on Venus to brave the heat, and far out near Pluto a signal is waiting that nobody can explain.

## The journey in six phases

1. **Earth industry** – ores, power, machines, crude oil and plastic.
2. **Space program** – research, the first probe, the first rocket, the Moon.
3. **Ring 1** – Moon, Mars and Venus, space suit and domes.
4. **Orbit** – your own space station and fusion power.
5. **Ring 2** – from Mercury to Pluto, with ever harsher conditions.
6. **Network and finale** – cargo rockets between your outposts and the starship.

## How to use this handbook

- The handbook grows with you: a new chapter opens as soon as you reach the matching research tier.
- Underlined terms are **links**. One click takes you to the matching page.
- Recipes are shown right on the machine pages. You find an overview under [[rezepte|All Recipes]].
- New to Infinite Horizon? Read the [[grundbegriffe|Basics]] first.
- Stuck? Look at [[erste_schritte|First Steps]] or the [[tipps|Tips and FAQ]].
